What the scanner can and cannot do

OCR can suggest the invoice reference, date, iPhone model, item price and invoice total when these are legible. Barcode/QR reading depends on browser support and what Apple encoded. Payment split, cash refund expectation, non-refundable cash and commission must still be entered by you. Never save a transaction until the invoice totals have been checked.

The photograph is processed in Safari using OCR software and is not sent to an invoice-processing service. The OCR engine and English language data are downloaded by your browser from a software CDN when first used.

Data and calculation rules

Cash expected = accessory refund expected minus refunds already received. Net cash outflow = cash paid minus cash refunds received. Non-refundable cash is separately recorded for the partner message. Commission is tracked separately from that amount. The app does not assume an expected refund or unpaid commission has been received.
